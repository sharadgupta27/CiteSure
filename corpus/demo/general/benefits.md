# Benefits Overview

## Health insurance

Acme Robotics pays 90 percent of the health insurance premium for employees and 70 percent for dependents. Dental and vision plans are included at no extra cost to the employee.

## Retirement

The company matches 401(k) contributions up to 4 percent of salary. The match vests immediately.

## Learning budget

Every employee has a learning budget of $1,500 per calendar year for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over to the next year. Conference travel is paid from the same budget unless a director approves an exception.

## Wellness

A wellness stipend of $40 per month covers a gym membership or similar activity. The employee assistance program offers free confidential counselling, available 24 hours a day.

## Commuter benefits

Employees can set aside pre-tax money for public transport and parking through the commuter benefit.
