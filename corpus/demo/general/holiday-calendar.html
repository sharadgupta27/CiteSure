Holiday Calendar 2026

Paid company holidays

Acme Robotics observes 10 paid company holidays in 2026.

  • New Year's Day on January 1
  • Martin Luther King Jr. Day on January 19
  • Memorial Day on May 25
  • Juneteenth on June 19
  • Independence Day, observed on July 3
  • Labor Day on September 7
  • Thanksgiving Day on November 26
  • The day after Thanksgiving on November 27
  • Christmas Eve on December 24
  • Christmas Day on December 25

Winter shutdown

The company is closed from December 28 to December 31. These days are not charged to your PTO balance.

Working on a holiday

Employees who must work on a company holiday, for example on-call engineers, receive a different day off within 30 days.
