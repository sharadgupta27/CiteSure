# Parental Leave Policy

## Who is eligible

All full-time employees who have worked at Acme Robotics for at least 6 months are eligible for paid parental leave. The policy applies to birth, adoption and foster placement.

## How much leave you get

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave is paid at 100 percent of base salary.

If you and your partner both work at Acme Robotics, each of you receives the leave that matches your caregiver status.

## When you can take it

Leave must be started within 12 months of the birth or placement. You may take the leave in one block or split it into two blocks, as long as each block is at least 2 weeks long.

## Returning to work

After leave, you can use a phased return. The phased return lasts up to 4 weeks, during which you work 60 percent of your normal hours at full pay.

## How to apply

Tell People Operations at least 30 days before your leave starts when possible. Health insurance and 401(k) matching continue unchanged during parental leave.
