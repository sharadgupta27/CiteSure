# Vacation and Paid Time Off

## Annual allowance

Every full-time employee receives 25 days of paid time off (PTO) per year. PTO accrues monthly at 2.08 days per month, starting on your first day.

## Carryover

You can carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Days above the limit expire on December 31.

## Requesting time off

Request time off in the HR portal. Requests longer than 5 consecutive working days must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance. Your manager answers requests within 3 business days.

## Public holidays

Company holidays are separate from PTO and do not reduce your balance. See the holiday calendar for the list of days.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is only required when you are away for more than 3 consecutive days. Sick days do not carry over.

## Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave of up to 30 days can be approved by your manager and People Operations for personal reasons.
