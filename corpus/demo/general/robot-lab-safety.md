# Robot Lab Safety

## Access

Only employees who completed the robot safety briefing may enter the lab. Visitors must be escorted at all times and wear a visitor badge.

## Protective equipment

Safety glasses are mandatory whenever a robot arm is powered. Closed shoes are required in the lab. Long hair and loose clothing must be tied back.

## Emergency stop

The emergency stop (e-stop) of every robot is tested at the start of each week and the result is logged in the safety register. A robot with a failed e-stop test must be tagged out of service immediately.

## Lifting

Robots and parts heavier than 20 kg need a two-person lift or a hoist. Never lift a heavy part alone.

## Reporting

Report every injury and every near miss to the safety officer within 24 hours. Near misses are reviewed in the monthly safety meeting.
