# On-Call Rotation

## Schedule

On-call rotations are one week long. The handoff happens every Monday at 10:00 in the team channel. No engineer is on call more than one week in every four weeks.

## Responsibilities

The on-call engineer must acknowledge a page within 10 minutes. If the engineer does not acknowledge in time, the page escalates to the secondary on-call and then to the engineering manager.

## Compensation

On-call engineers receive a stipend of $350 per week. Pages handled between 22:00 and 07:00 earn an extra $75 per incident.

## Time off after a night page

An engineer paged for more than 2 hours during the night may start work 4 hours later the next day without using PTO.

## Swapping shifts

Swaps are allowed if both engineers agree and the swap is recorded in the on-call calendar at least 24 hours before the shift starts.
