# Onboarding Guide

## Before day one

IT ships your laptop to arrive three days before your start date. Your manager creates your accounts and assigns you an onboarding buddy.

## First week

On day one you meet your team, collect your badge and set up your accounts. Security awareness training must be completed within your first week. You also complete the robot safety briefing before entering the lab.

## The 30-60-90 plan

By day 30 you finish your onboarding tasks and ship a small change. By day 60 you own a small piece of work from start to finish. By day 90 you present what you learned to your team and agree on goals with your manager.

## Onboarding buddy

Your buddy is a peer, not your manager. Meet your buddy twice a week during the first month. Ask any question, however small.

## Probation

The first 90 days are a probation period. During this period either side can end the employment with one week of notice.
