# IT Equipment

## Laptops

Employees choose a Mac or a Linux laptop. Laptops are replaced every 36 months. Engineers who work with simulation software may request a workstation with a GPU.

## Accessories

Each employee has a $400 budget for a monitor and accessories, which can be used once every three years.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen device to IT within 24 hours. IT will lock and wipe the device remotely.

## Leaving the company

Return all company equipment within 10 days of your last day. IT deactivates your accounts at the end of your last working day.

## Software

Install software only from the approved catalog. If you need something that is not in the catalog, request it through the IT portal.
