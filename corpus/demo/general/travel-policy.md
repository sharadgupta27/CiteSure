# Business Travel Policy

## Booking

Book flights and hotels through the travel portal at least 14 days before departure. Late bookings need manager approval.

## Flights

Economy class is the default for flights shorter than 6 hours. For flights of 6 hours or longer, you may book premium economy. Business class is not permitted unless a VP approves it in writing.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is $220 in major cities and $180 everywhere else. Choose hotels within 3 kilometres of the work location when possible.

## Ground transport

Use public transport or ride sharing for short trips. Rent a mid-size car only when public transport is not practical.

## International travel

The meal per-diem for international trips is $60 per day. Travelers must carry the emergency contact card issued by People Operations.

## Travel safety

Check the government travel advisory for your destination. Acme Robotics does not approve trips to destinations rated level 4 (do not travel).
