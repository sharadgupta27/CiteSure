# Expense Policy

## Submitting expenses

Submit expense reports in Spendly within 30 days of the purchase. Reports submitted later than 60 days will not be reimbursed.

## Receipts

A receipt is required for every expense above $25. For smaller expenses, a short note in Spendly is enough.

## Approvals

Expenses up to $500 are approved by your manager. Expenses between $500 and $5,000 need approval from your director. Anything above $5,000 needs approval from a VP before you spend the money.

## Meals

When you travel for work, meals are reimbursed up to $75 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Corporate cards

Cardholders must reconcile their card transactions by the 5th business day of each month. Corporate cards are for business expenses only.

## Software purchases

Do not buy software with a personal card. Request it through the IT portal so that security can review the vendor.
