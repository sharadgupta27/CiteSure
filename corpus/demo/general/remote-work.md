# Remote and Hybrid Work

## Hybrid schedule

Acme Robotics follows a hybrid model. Employees in roles that do not need the robotics lab come to the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays. The other three days can be worked from home.

## Core hours

Everyone is expected to be reachable during core hours, which are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone.

## Working from another country

You may work from another country for up to 20 days per calendar year. The request needs written approval from your manager and from People Operations at least 30 days before you travel. Working abroad without approval can create tax and legal problems for you and for the company.

## Home office support

New employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $500 for a desk, chair or monitor. The company also pays $50 per month toward home internet.

## Lab roles

Hardware engineers and technicians work on site in the robotics lab at least four days per week because they need access to physical robots.
