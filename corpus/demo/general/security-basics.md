# Information Security Basics

## Passwords and MFA

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Use the company password manager to create and store them. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company accounts.

## Devices

Lock your screen when you leave your desk. Laptops lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. All laptops use full-disk encryption, which IT enables before handing you the device.

## Phishing

Report suspected phishing emails to security@acme.example within 1 hour. Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious messages. Reporting is never punished, even if you already clicked.

## Public networks

Use the company VPN whenever you connect from a public Wi-Fi network such as a cafe or an airport.

## Removable media

Personal USB drives must not be plugged into company laptops. Use the approved file-sharing tool to move files.

## Data classification

Company data is labelled Public, Internal or Restricted. Restricted data, such as customer contracts and employee records, may only be shared with people who need it for their work.
