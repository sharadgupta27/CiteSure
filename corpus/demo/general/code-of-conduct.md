# Code of Conduct

## Our principles

We treat each other with respect. Harassment, discrimination and bullying are not tolerated in the office, in the lab or online.

## Reporting a concern

You can report a concern to your manager, to People Operations or to the anonymous ethics hotline. People Operations acknowledges every report within 2 business days.

## Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is prohibited and is treated as a serious violation by itself.

## Conflicts of interest

Disclose any outside business, board seat or financial interest that may conflict with your work at Acme Robotics. Gifts from vendors worth more than $100 must be declined or reported.

## Using company resources

Company equipment and accounts are for company work. Limited personal use is fine as long as it does not break the law or this code.
