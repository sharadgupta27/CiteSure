# Severance and Layoff Procedure (Confidential)

## Severance pay

Severance is 2 weeks of base pay for each full year of service. The minimum severance is 4 weeks and the maximum is 26 weeks.

## Notice

Employees affected by a layoff receive 60 days of written notice or pay in place of notice. Group layoffs follow the WARN Act rules.

## Benefits after leaving

Health insurance continues for 3 months after the last day at the company's expense. Unused PTO is paid out in the final paycheck.

## Approval

Any layoff affecting more than 10 employees needs approval from the CEO and the board.

## Communication

Managers must not discuss a planned layoff with their teams. People Operations prepares the talking points and the exit packages.
