# Workplace Investigation Procedure (Confidential)

## Starting an investigation

People Operations opens a case file within 2 business days of a report. Investigations involving an executive are handled by external counsel.

## Interviews

The investigator interviews the person who reported the concern within 5 business days. The person who is the subject of the report is interviewed afterwards and may bring a colleague for support.

## Timeline

A written finding is due within 30 days of opening the case. Complex cases may be extended by 15 days with the approval of the Chief People Officer.

## Confidentiality

Case files are stored in a restricted folder and are shared only on a need-to-know basis. Witnesses are asked not to discuss the case.

## Outcomes

Possible outcomes are no action, coaching, a written warning or termination. The reporting person is told that the case is closed, but not the details of any disciplinary action.
