# Performance Improvement Plan Procedure (Confidential)

## When to start a plan

A performance improvement plan (PIP) may start only after at least two documented coaching conversations. People Operations must approve every PIP before it is shared with the employee.

## Length and structure

A PIP lasts 60 days. The manager and the employee hold a check-in every week. The plan lists three to five measurable goals.

## Decision at the end

At day 60, the manager and People Operations decide whether the employee has met the goals. The possible outcomes are: the plan is closed successfully, the plan is extended once by 30 days, or the employment ends.

## Documentation

Every check-in is written down and signed by both sides within 2 business days. Missing documentation can invalidate a termination decision.
