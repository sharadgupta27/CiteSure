# Salary Bands (Confidential)

This document is restricted to People Operations. Do not share it with employees or managers.

## Engineering bands for 2026

Level L3 has a base salary range of $95,000 to $120,000. Level L4 has a range of $120,000 to $150,000. Level L5 has a range of $150,000 to $190,000. Level L6 has a range of $190,000 to $240,000.

## Bonus targets

The annual bonus target is 10 percent of base salary for levels L3 and L4. The target is 15 percent for levels L5 and above.

## Equity refresh

Equity refresh grants are reviewed once a year in March. Employees rated "exceeds expectations" receive a refresh of 0.02 percent for L5 and above.

## Exceptions

An offer above the top of a band needs approval from the Chief People Officer.
