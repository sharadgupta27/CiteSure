# Q3 2026 Financial Results: Board Pack Summary

> Fictional sample data for CiteSure. Fernhollow Bicycle Works is an invented company. Classification: Confidential,
> Finance group only. Not for distribution before the results are announced.

Prepared by Finance for the board meeting on 14 October 2026. Quarter: 1 July to 30 September 2026.

## Headline numbers

| Measure | Q3 2026 | Q3 2025 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | £8.4 million | £7.1 million | +18 percent |
| Gross margin | 38.2 percent | 36.5 percent | +1.7 points |
| EBITDA | £0.9 million | £0.6 million | +50 percent |
| Cash at quarter end | £3.1 million | £2.7 million | +15 percent |
| Headcount at quarter end | 142 | 131 | +11 |

## Commentary

Revenue growth came mainly from e-bike sales to dealers in Germany and the Netherlands, which grew 41 percent. Direct
online sales in the UK were flat. Gross margin improved because aluminium tube prices fell 6 percent after the new
contract with Ardent Tube Co. took effect on 1 April 2026.

Pre-orders for the unreleased Trailhawk 2027 reached 1,850 bikes by 30 September, against a launch plan of 1,500.

## Risks

- **Fork supply** for the Trailhawk could limit Q1 2027 deliveries to 1,500 bikes (see the engineering release checklist).
- **Currency:** 46 percent of component purchases are paid in US dollars; a 5 percent fall in sterling would reduce
  annual gross profit by about £280,000.
- **Warranty:** the warranty provision was raised by £60,000 after a batch of rear derailleur hangers was recalled.

## Outlook

Finance expects full-year 2026 revenue of £31 to 32 million and EBITDA of £3.2 million. The board is asked to approve a
capital budget of £450,000 for a second fatigue test rig.
