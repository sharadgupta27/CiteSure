# Fernhollow Bicycle Works: IT Security Policy

> Fictional sample data for CiteSure. Fernhollow Bicycle Works is an invented company. Classification: Internal.

Version 2.0, approved by the leadership team on 12 May 2026. Owner: IT and Security.

## Accounts and passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Use the company password manager; never reuse a work password anywhere
else. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the HR portal, the ERP system and the source code host.
Accounts are locked after 10 failed sign-in attempts and unlocked by the IT service desk.

## Devices

Company laptops use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Install software only
from the company software centre. Personal USB storage devices are not allowed on company laptops or workshop machines;
use the shared drive or the file-transfer service instead. A lost or stolen device must be reported within 1 hour.

## Data classification

| Level | Examples | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Public | catalogue, press releases | may be shared freely |
| Internal | this handbook, team plans | employees and approved contractors only |
| Confidential | supplier prices, unreleased designs, financial results | named groups only; never in personal email |
| Restricted | salaries, health data, passwords | named people only; encrypted at rest and in transit |

Confidential and Restricted information must never be pasted into public AI tools, translation websites or search
engines.

## Reporting incidents

Report a suspected security incident (phishing email, malware warning, lost device, data sent to the wrong person) to
security@fernhollow.example or phone extension 4400 within 1 hour of noticing it. Do not try to investigate on your
own, and do not delete the suspicious email: forward it as an attachment.

## Remote work

Use the company VPN on any network outside our sites. Do not work on Confidential or Restricted documents in public
places where your screen can be seen.
