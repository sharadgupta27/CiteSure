# Fernhollow Bicycle Works: Employee Handbook

> Fictional sample data for CiteSure. Fernhollow Bicycle Works is an invented company. Classification: Internal.

Version 4.2, effective 1 January 2026. Owner: People Operations.

## Working hours

Our standard week is 37.5 hours. Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00, Monday to Friday; outside core hours you may start and
finish when it suits you, as long as your team agrees. Workshop staff on the assembly line work fixed shifts: the early
shift runs 06:00 to 14:00 and the late shift runs 14:00 to 22:00.

## Annual leave

Every employee receives 27 days of paid annual leave per year, plus UK bank holidays. Leave is pro-rated for part-time
staff. You may carry over up to 5 unused days into the next year; carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they
are lost. Book leave in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for anything longer than three days.

## Sickness

If you are ill, tell your manager by 09:30 on the first day of absence (or one hour before your shift starts). For
absences of more than seven calendar days you need a fit note from a doctor. Fernhollow pays full salary for the first
10 days of sickness absence in any rolling 12 months, then statutory sick pay.

## Parental leave

The primary carer receives 16 weeks of parental leave at full pay, followed by up to 23 weeks at statutory pay. The
second parent receives 4 weeks at full pay, which may be taken at any time in the first year. Tell People Operations at
least 15 weeks before the expected week of birth or placement.

## Probation

New employees serve a probation period of 3 months. Your manager holds a review at the end of month two; probation can
be extended once, by up to 3 more months.

## Benefits

- **Ride-to-work allowance:** £40 per month, paid with salary, for anyone who cycles to work at least twice a week.
- **Staff discount:** 35 percent off Fernhollow bikes and 20 percent off parts and clothing, up to two bikes per year.
- **Pension:** Fernhollow contributes 6 percent of salary when you contribute at least 4 percent.
- **Learning budget:** £750 per employee per year for courses, books and conferences, approved by your manager.
