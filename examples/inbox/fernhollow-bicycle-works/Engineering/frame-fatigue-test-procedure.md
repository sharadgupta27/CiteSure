# Frame Fatigue Test Procedure (ENG-TP-014)

> Fictional sample data for CiteSure. Fernhollow Bicycle Works is an invented company. Classification: Confidential,
> Engineering only.

Revision C, 3 September 2026. Owner: Test Lab lead.

## Purpose

This procedure checks that every production batch of aluminium and steel frames survives repeated pedalling loads. It
is based on the fatigue tests in ISO 4210-6, with stricter internal limits.

## Sampling

Take 3 frames at random from every production batch of up to 400 frames. Frames from a new tube supplier, or from a
new welding jig, need 5 samples for the first three batches.

## Pedalling fatigue test

1. Mount the frame in test rig FT-2 with the dummy fork and bottom-bracket adapter.
2. Apply an alternating pedalling force of 1,200 N on each side, at no more than 10 Hz.
3. Run 100,000 cycles. Inspect the frame every 25,000 cycles with dye penetrant around the head tube, bottom bracket and
   rear dropouts.

## Pass criteria

A frame passes when it completes 100,000 cycles with no visible crack, no broken weld and a permanent deflection below
2 mm at the bottom bracket. All 3 sampled frames must pass for the batch to be released.

## When a frame fails

If any sampled frame fails, the whole batch is put into quarantine in bay Q3 and may not be shipped. The Test Lab
opens a non-conformance report within 24 hours, and Quality decides within 5 working days whether the batch is
retested, reworked or scrapped. The tube supplier is informed through the 8D process in the supplier quality agreement.

## Records

Store the rig log, photos and dye-penetrant results in the test database under the batch number. Keep records for 10
years.
