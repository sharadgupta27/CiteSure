## Product summary

The Trailhawk 2027 is a hardtail trail bike with a 6061 aluminium frame, 130 mm suspension fork and 29-inch wheels. Target
retail price is £1,649. The frame weighs 1.82 kg in size medium.

## Open issues

1. **Rear dropout tolerance:** the first prototype frames from the new jig measured 0.3 mm outside tolerance at the rear
   dropout. Fix: re-shim jig J-7; recheck on the next 10 frames. Owner: Manufacturing Engineering. Must close before BOM
   freeze.
2. **Fork supply:** the fork supplier has confirmed 1,500 units for Q1 2027 against a forecast of 2,000. Purchasing is
   negotiating a second delivery in February.
3. **Paint:** the "Moss Green" colour failed the salt-spray test at 240 hours (target 480 hours). A new primer is being
   tested.

## Release criteria

- Frame passes ENG-TP-014 fatigue testing on all 5 samples from the first three batches.
- No open issue rated "safety" or "high".
- Assembly time at or below 95 minutes per bike on the pilot build.
- Service manual and spare-parts list published to dealers 30 days before launch.
